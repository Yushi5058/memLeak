You noticed it first.
Shadows moved wrong across the classroom
wall.

Minutes passed. Seasons ran down like
tape.
You counted the years and stopped
counting.

So you went looking for where time was
leaking out.
You found the wound. It did not let you
leave.

And it is very quiet here.
The dust has not moved in a hundred
years.

Every second you stand still, the Earth
loses a little more.
So do not stand still.
Reach the gate. Nothing else matters.
