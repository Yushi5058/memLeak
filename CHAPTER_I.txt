The wound runs
deeper than the
wall. You climb
into the spine
of the thing.

Cargo. Rows of
it, stamped with
years, waiting
for a planet
that is still
losing them.

The crates move.
So does the
floor. You stop
counting years
and start
climbing.

Whatever they
stored here,
they did not
store it for
us.

Keep moving.
The gate is up.
